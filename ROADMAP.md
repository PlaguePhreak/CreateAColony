# Create a Colony: roadmap

Create a Colony brings the ideas of the Minecraft mod MineColonies to Vintage Story. This page shows what is done, what is being worked on, and where the mod could go next. Plans can change as things get tested in game.

## Done

**Getting started (0.1 to 0.5)**
- Town Hall and a Supply Camp to start a colony
- Settlers who eat, sleep, shelter from storms and come back after dying (or not, with Permadeath)
- Warehouse, drop-off points and the Courier job
- Builder's Hut, blueprints, work orders and an "items needed" list
- Importing MineColonies blueprints with `/colony importmc`

**Working settlers (0.6 to 0.9)**
- Builders need tools, make fire pits, and can use any wood or stone type
- Settler inventories with bags, tool animations and a settler window
- Forester (cuts and replants trees) and Farmer (tills, sows, waters, harvests)
- Hut storage and a Work area tab for how far workers go
- Guards who escort workers and fight monsters
- Town Hall model from a friend

**Placing and new jobs (0.10 to 0.17)**
- Builder's Wand with a placement preview, turning and nudging buildings
- Potter who shapes clay and fires it in a pit kiln
- Farmer's Hut model
- Tavern, wanderers who ask to join, and hiring recruits
- Settlers eat real meals and like a varied diet
- Settlers open and close doors and gates
- Cook's Hut: the Cook grills food and makes pot meals
- Builders copy VS Roofing roofs

**Better buildings and a bigger colony (0.18 to 0.28)**
- New Builder's Hut, plus cottages for the Forester's Hut and Tavern
- Builder's Wand tabs by category, and Testing / Dev's Builds styles
- Paths, ladder climbing and help for stuck settlers
- Colony land that grows as huts are built
- Guard Tower, guard tasks and armor
- MineColonies-style Town Hall window with an event log, work orders, stats and a town map
- Furniture and banners in blueprints, and colony banners on buildings
- Aaron's Town Hall and Builder's Hut, levels 1 to 4
- See-through ghost preview of the real blueprint while placing
- Families and the Residence: couples, children who grow up, surnames
- Outlines of nearby buildings while placing, with a warning on overlaps

## In progress or drafted

- **Settler traits and skills**: a first draft of how settlers get better at their jobs
- **Blacksmith's Hut**: an idea for a smith who makes and repairs tools and armor
- **Cook's Hut levels 2 to 4**, and cooking with food mods (A Culinary Artillery, Expanded Foods)
- **More buildings** for the empty Builder's Wand tabs: Education, Walls and Decoration
- **Level 2 to 4 upgrades** for the huts that only have level 1
- **Custom models** for the huts that still look like plain blocks

## Near future

- Finish the drafts above, one at a time, tested in game
- Polish pass: tidy windows, fix rough edges found while testing
- Bigger and better blueprints for every hut

## Mid future

- More jobs from MineColonies, such as a Fisher, Miner, Hunter, Shepherd and Smelter
- Schools and training buildings for the Education tab
- Ranged guards, a Barracks and colony walls
- Working with more popular Vintage Story mods

## Far future: AI settlements

Colonies that run themselves, without a player in charge.

- **The Mayor**: a settler role that does the player's job. The Mayor chooses where new buildings go, places them with the Builder's Wand, hands out jobs, hires wanderers and upgrades huts when the colony is ready.
- **Growing on their own**: AI settlements that start from a Town Hall and grow into villages and towns over time.
- **Living in the world**: settlements that appear in the world, so players can find them, visit them, trade with them or join them.
- **Neighbours**: AI settlements that trade with, help or argue with the player's colony and each other.
- **War and diplomacy**: a combat system so settlements can raise armies, go to war, defend their land and take or lose territory. Settlements can also form alliances, make peace, sign trade deals and send envoys, with the Mayor deciding who is a friend and who is an enemy.
