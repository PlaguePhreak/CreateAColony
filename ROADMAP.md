# Vintage Colonies: roadmap

Vintage Colonies brings the ideas of the Minecraft mod MineColonies to Vintage Story. This page shows what the mod has today (version 0.154.0), what it does not have yet, and the order we plan to build it in. It comes from a side-by-side check against the MineColonies wiki. Plans can change as things get tested in game.

Things marked *(tested)* have been tried in game. Everything else is built but still waiting for an in-game test.

## What we have

**Huts and jobs**
- Town Hall: founds the colony, claims land, levels, a MineColonies-style window *(tested)*
- Builder's Hut: work orders, an items-needed list, tools that wear down, VS Roofing roofs *(tested)*
- Residence: beds per level, couples and children *(tested)*
- Warehouse, drop-off points and the Courier *(tested)*
- Tavern: recruits for rusty gears, and wanderers who ask to join *(tested)*
- Supply Camp with a starter kit and the Colony Ledger *(tested)*
- Farmer: fields, weeding, watering, fertilizer, crop rotation, berry bushes and fruit trees *(tested)*. Also chases off animals that eat the crops (or calls the hunter), only plants crops that will survive the season, skips winter planting, and holds the watering can while watering (0.147.0-0.152.0, not tested yet)
- Forester: fells wild trees and replants them *(tested)*. Also clears wild bushes for sticks, splits firewood into sticks, and leaves fruit trees alone unless you allow them (0.146.0-0.148.0, not tested yet)
- Miner: a MineColonies-style shaft, digs the Prospector's ore finds, carries torches, fills old mines back in *(tested)*
- Quarry: a stepped pit for stone *(tested)*
- Cook: grilling and pot meals, harder meals as the Cooking skill grows *(tested)*
- Guard Tower: guard, patrol and follow tasks, night watch, alarms, retreat and armor *(tested)*
- Barracks: a bigger guard post, two guards per level, with a wider watch and patrol (0.54.0, placeholder model and building, not tested yet)
- Guards swap in better weapons, armor and shields from their tower and the warehouse, and block with shields (0.149.0, not tested yet)
- Our own jobs: the Prospector (finds ore and marks the map) and the Potter (shapes clay and fires pit kilns) *(tested)*
- Smelter's Hut: melts nuggets in a crucible over the fire pit and pours ingots into molds (0.50.0, placeholder model and building, not tested yet)
- Blacksmith's Hut: heats ingots in the forge and makes tool heads and finished tools on the anvil (0.50.0, placeholder model and building, not tested yet)
- Sawmill: a sawyer saws logs into planks and makes other wooden things from the game's recipes (0.52.0, placeholder model and building, not tested yet)
- Primitive Craftsman: a craftsman knaps flint and stones into tool heads and puts them on sticks, making stone tools for the colony (0.103.0, placeholder model and building, not tested yet)
- Tailor's Hut and Jeweller's Workshop: a tailor makes and mends clothes and leather or linen armor, a jeweller makes jewellery from our own recipes; clothes wear out, and cold or ragged settlers are unhappy (0.113.0, placeholder models, no buildings yet, not tested yet)
- Armourer's Hut and Weaponsmith's Hut: the Blacksmith split in three; the armourer makes and mends metal armor, the weaponsmith makes weapons (0.114.0, placeholder models, no buildings yet, not tested yet)
- Repair button on every hut: a builder puts the building back as its blueprint has it, takes out stray blocks and fills holes under it (0.118.0, not tested yet)
- Healing: badly hurt settlers go home to bed to recover and use a bandage or poultice they carry; a healer makes bandages and treats them (Healer's Station in a Church since 0.137.0); settlers make horsetail poultices; the Gatherer picks horsetail (0.117.0, placeholder model, no building yet, not tested yet)
- Hunter's Hut: a hunter hunts wild animals and brings the meat and hides home; jobless settlers skin animals and cook simple meals at home (0.99.0, not tested yet)
- Explorer's Hut and Gatherer's Hut: an explorer marks ore, clay, traders, animals and danger on a Colony markers map layer, and a gatherer forages berries, mushrooms and loose stones and marks plants; works with More Map Icons (0.77.0, placeholder models and buildings, not tested yet). The gatherer also takes ore bits, wild crops, clay, peat, seashells, reeds, herbs and grass for dry grass, set on its Allowed to collect tab (0.91.0-0.121.0, not tested yet). Since then it also makes poultices, marks what it sees as it walks, has an Only look around mode, a Priorities tab for what to collect first, digs and roasts roots when food runs low, and takes bush cuttings (0.125.0-0.151.0, not tested yet)
- Vintage Colonies' own map icons: 120 waypoint icons covering every More Map Icons sheet entry, used by colony markers (0.79.0, not tested yet)
- A marker icon for every ore, fruit, vegetable, grain, animal, trader and story location, other mods' too (0.80.0, not tested yet)
- Remove a colony marker by right-clicking it on the world map; settlers leave that spot alone after (0.126.0, not tested yet)
- The Bailiff: answers wanderers, gives jobless settlers work, makes farmers work faster, collects rent, keeps a ledger and an estate report on a new Bailiff tab, and moves stuck workers to another hut for a while (0.127.0 and 0.129.0, not tested yet)
- Courier's Station: a work station inside the Warehouse; huts ask for a few spare, and kilns and forges pick the best fuel (0.142.0, not tested yet)
- New Dev's Builds huts from Aaron: Smelter 1, Blacksmith 1, Sawmill 1 and 2, Gatherer 1, and Small and Large Forester 1 (0.134.0, not tested yet)
- Royal Mint and the economy: a minter strikes copper coins; on the first of each month the bailiff collects taxes (rate set on the Bailiff tab, higher is less happy) and pays each settler a wage for their job; unpaid settlers are unhappy (0.136.0, not tested yet); penny-style coin model, and a coin designed with the Coinage mod can be the colony's money (0.141.0, not tested yet); taxes and wages only from Town Hall level 3 (0.153.0)
- Church and religion: priests and a bishop, Faith, services every 7 days (happier, learn faster, small chance of a skill level), tithe, charity (food for the hungry, bandages, orphans, beds for the homeless), funerals, record books, 5 church paintings the priest hangs; work stations, starting with the Healer's Station inside a Church in place of the Healer's Hut (0.137.0, placeholder model, no building yet, not tested yet); with no Church in the colony there are no church effects at all (0.154.0)
- Paintings: 17 original framed paintings in four sizes, a blank canvas from 8 boards and 4 linen painted with buckets of dye in the grid, hung by builders from blueprints (0.105.0-0.105.1, not tested yet)

**Colony systems**
- Cellar storage, settlers eat the oldest food first, and a Kitchen tab with food on hand, crocks and settler bowls (0.100.0 and 0.124.0, not tested yet)
- Huts make what other huts need, a Crafting recipes tab and a Priorities tab on crafting huts (0.101.0-0.111.0, not tested yet)
- Crafting recipes tab on every hut, and Teach recipe to add your own recipes (0.140.0, not tested yet)
- Settlers decorate their homes with paintings, trophies, weapons, books and banners (0.106.0, not tested yet)
- Traders join the colony as settlers with a job that fits their goods (0.108.0, not tested yet)
- Colony land that grows with every hut and Town Hall level *(tested)*
- Town Hall window: actions, town map, banners, events, work orders, citizens, statistics, permissions, settings, prospecting and quests *(tested)*
- Settlers who eat real meals, like a varied diet, sleep in beds, shelter from storms and come back after dying (or not, with Permadeath) *(tested)*
- Firefighting: settlers put out fires on the colony's land with watering cans, and the potter clears grass around its pit kiln first (0.123.0, not tested yet)
- Skills (13) and traits, inherited by children *(tested)*
- RimWorld-style skills 0 to 20 with passions, decay and perks, and a Happiness system *(0.61.0, not tested yet)*
- Families: couples, children who grow up, surnames, a population cap *(tested)*
- Requests: workers ask for missing items, couriers fetch them, guard towers keep a minimum stock *(tested)*
- Hut levels, ordered from each hut's window, with the Medieval Oak packs converted from Aaron's MineColonies blueprints (0.48.0) and Aaron's Dev's Builds *(tested)*
- Placing huts by hand without the wand or a colony, for making blueprints (`/colony handplace`, 0.49.0)
- Builder's Wand with a see-through preview, turning, nudging, level preview and outlines of nearby buildings *(tested)*
- Importing MineColonies blueprints with `/colony importmc` *(tested)*
- Settlers run from attackers to home or a guard *(tested)*, and fight back when cornered (0.76.0, not tested yet)
- Mineshaft Entrance with mine levels, ladders between them and a Levels tab; Remove hut for any building; old mines left derelict (0.76.0-0.96.0, not tested yet)
- Colony militia tab, and settlers shown on the world map (0.85.0, not tested yet)
- Settler Stats tab, and choosing who lives in each home (0.74.0-0.75.0, not tested yet)
- Settlers move like players: torch pose, idle fidgets, sitting to eat and on breaks (0.83.0); swimming, the player's ladder climb, sprinting, greeting players, cheering or crying by mood, shivering in the cold and holding food to eat (0.131.0); not tested yet
- Every grown settler carries a torch or lantern in the dark, and leaves lanterns for builds first (0.144.0, not tested yet)
- Settlers get into their beds even when the path is awkward, and don't share a bed with strangers when one is free (0.128.0, not tested yet)
- Corpses for players and settlers, grave markers on the map, and the cause of death in the settler list (0.77.0-0.94.0, not tested yet)
- Town map shows all loaded land with free zoom (0.78.0, not tested yet)
- Jobless settlers tend crops at home and help with hauling (0.53.0, not tested yet)
- Wattle and daub building, a house type picker and Aaron's Dev's Builds houses and Cook's Huts (0.56.0-0.60.0, not tested yet)
- Roads, ladder climbing, doors and gates, and Willpower for stuck settlers *(tested)*
- Colony banners, furniture in blueprints, a quest book, and settlers who look like players and wear clothes and armor *(tested)*

## Partly done

- **Cook's Hut**: baking comes from the Stone Bake Oven mod (0.55.0); still no place where the Cook serves food, no food-mod cooking stations *(tested)*
- **Town Hall window**: alliances, happiness, reordering work orders, custom ranks, permission logs, auto hiring and housing, and enter and leave messages say "Coming later" *(tested)*
- **Permissions**: no custom ranks, no hostile or neutral list, no log of who did what *(tested)*
- **Requests**: every hut has a Requests tab (0.142.0); still no way to fill a request from that list (the Required resources tab has a give button)
- **Hiring and housing**: no auto-hire switch (choosing who lives in each home came in 0.75.0)
- **Guards**: no ranged guards yet; the Barracks came in 0.54.0 and the militia tab in 0.85.0 *(not tested yet)*
- **Blueprints**: no in-game scan tool for your own buildings, and the wand's Education, Walls and Decoration tabs are still empty *(tested)*
- **Death**: corpses, grave markers and causes of death came in 0.77.0-0.94.0, and the priest holds funerals at graves (0.137.0), but there is no graveyard yet
- **Quests**: the quest book works, but Tavern jobs and settler requests are not in it yet *(tested)*
- **Stash and Postbox**: drop-off points exist, but there is no postbox to order items to the player *(tested)*

## Not built yet

**Next, in this order**
1. **Smelter** (started in 0.50.0): still to do are alloys such as bronze, and a bloomery for iron
2. **Happiness** came in 0.61.0. Still to add: safety (raids), health, and how crowded the colony is
3. **A request list**: seeing what workers need came in 0.142.0 (Requests tab). Still to add: filling a request from the tab
4. **Animal herders and a Tanner**: chickens, pigs, sheep and goats in pens for meat, eggs and hides, and a Tanner who makes leather in barrels. Jobless settlers can also keep a few animals in their own gardens, mostly chickens, the way many 13th and 14th century households did, feeding them and collecting the eggs.
5. **Butcher's Hut**: a butcher takes the animals the Hunter (0.99.0) and herders bring home and cuts them up for meat, hides, fat and bones, then stores the meat with the food from 0.100.0. Works with the **Butchering** mod by CaptainOats when it is installed: the butcher hangs carcasses on its hooks, uses its tables, catches blood in buckets and smokes meat on its racks for more food per animal. Without the mod, the butcher uses a knife like the game does. The mod lists no licence, so we will never copy or bundle its files. It stays an optional extra that players download themselves.
6. **Ranged guards and a rally banner** (the Barracks came in 0.54.0), then **Raids**: now and then, enemies turn up and attack the player's colony. The player is warned in chat and on the Town Hall window. There are several raid factions, from bandits (who use the settler models) to the game's own monsters such as drifters and shivers.
7. **Graveyard**: an undertaker who buries the dead (the Healer, bandages and poultices came in 0.117.0)
8. **School, Library and University** with a **research tree** that unlocks faster work and new huts
9. **Blacksmith**: mostly done. Armor, mending and the Weaponsmith came in 0.114.0, and huts make what workers ask for since 0.111.0. Still to do: the Armourer's and Weaponsmith's buildings

**Later**
- **Economy**: settlers spend their coins (a market, the Tavern, buying furniture and clothes), coins of other metals, fines and market fees for the Bailiff. The Royal Mint, taxes and wages came in 0.136.0.
- More huts from MineColonies: Stonemason, Baker, Fisher, Beekeeper, Plantation (reeds and cattails), Composter, Crusher and Sifter (quern and panning), Florist, Dyer, Fletcher and Mechanic (windmills and gears)
- A Combat Academy and an archery range to train guards
- Charcoal burner for the Smelter and Blacksmith
- Settler chat bubbles where settlers ask for things or complain
- Walls, wells, roads and other decorations to order from the Builder's Wand
- **Outposts**: a ready-made bundle of buildings that can be placed outside the colony's border. Its buildings level up all together or one at a time. An outpost works as its own small base and tries to look after itself. It keeps a small military force away from home to protect workers out in the field, and gives them a safe place to go when they are attacked, or at night when the outpost is closer than home.
- **Bailiff (rest) and Scribe**: the Bailiff came in 0.127.0. Still to do: the Bailiff stops criminals and gives orders to the guards, and collects fines and market fees once there is trading. The Scribe lives in the Town Hall too and keeps a record of the colony's history in books they make themselves.
- **Alchemist's Hut**: a new Alchemist settler who works with the **Alchemy** mod by llama3013 when it is installed. The Alchemist grows and gathers herbs, dries them on herb racks, grinds them with a mortar and pestle, and brews potions in the brewing cauldron. Guards get fighting and healing flasks, miners get Miner's Flasks, hunters get Hunter's Delight, and the Healer gets Healing Oil. The mod uses the CC BY 4.0 licence, so we may reuse its work if we credit the author. We still plan to keep it an optional mod that players download themselves.
- Working with more popular Vintage Story mods, such as cooking with A Culinary Artillery and Expanded Foods

**Left out on purpose**
- Concrete Mixer, Enchanter, Netherworker and the Mystical Site: Vintage Story has nothing to match them. (The Alchemist is now planned above, using the Alchemy mod.)

## Far future: AI settlements

Colonies that run themselves, without a player in charge.

- **The Mayor**: a settler role that does the player's job. The Mayor chooses where new buildings go, places them with the Builder's Wand, hands out jobs, hires wanderers and upgrades huts when the colony is ready.
- **Growing on their own**: AI settlements that start from a Town Hall and grow into villages and towns over time.
- **Living in the world**: settlements that appear in the world, so players can find them, visit them, trade with them or join them.
- **Neighbours**: AI settlements that trade with, help or argue with the player's colony and each other.
- **War and diplomacy**: a combat system so settlements can raise armies, go to war, defend their land and take or lose territory. Settlements can also form alliances, make peace, sign trade deals and send envoys, with the Mayor deciding who is a friend and who is an enemy.
- **War camps**: before an attack, an AI settlement builds a war camp near the enemy colony and gathers its forces there.
